Frequently Asked Questions

Is CryoPen treatment painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. You may feel a slight stinging or cold sensation during treatment, which typically lasts only a few seconds. The treatment is generally well-tolerated, even by children aged 6 and up.

How long does the treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the size and number of lesions. Most single lesions can be treated in just a few seconds to a couple of minutes. A typical appointment, including consultation, takes 15-30 minutes.

What is the healing time?

Healing time varies from 1-4 weeks depending on the lesion type, size, and location. During this time, the treated area will form a scab which will naturally fall off as new skin forms underneath.

Will there be scarring?

CryoPen treatment typically results in minimal to no scarring when proper aftercare is followed. The risk of scarring is significantly lower than traditional surgical removal methods.

How many treatments will I need?

Most lesions can be successfully removed in a single treatment session. However, some larger or more stubborn lesions may require a follow-up treatment after healing.

Can all skin lesions be treated with CryoPen?

CryoPen is suitable for most benign skin lesions including skin tags, warts, age spots, and more. During your consultation, Devon will assess your lesion to ensure CryoPen is the appropriate treatment.

Is CryoPen safe for children?

Yes! CryoPen is safe for children aged 6 and above. Devon has specialized training in child-friendly care and uses techniques to ensure young patients feel comfortable and safe throughout the treatment.

What aftercare is required?

Aftercare is simple and straightforward. Keep the area clean and dry, avoid picking at any scabs, and protect from sun exposure. Devon will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions following your treatment.
