What is Lip Enhancement?

Lip enhancement treatments use hyaluronic acid-based fillers to add volume, define shape, and create beautiful, balanced lips. Whether you desire subtle enhancement or fuller, more prominent lips, our expert practitioners customize each treatment to complement your facial features and achieve your aesthetic goals.

From traditional lip fillers to innovative treatments like the lip flip and lip boosters, we offer a range of options to enhance your natural beauty. Our philosophy focuses on creating natural-looking results that maintain your lip movement and expression while providing the fullness and definition you desire.

Results are immediate and typically last 6-12 months, with many clients returning for maintenance treatments to maintain their enhanced appearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my lips look natural?

Yes! Our goal is to enhance your natural features, not create an overdone appearance. We use conservative amounts and advanced techniques to ensure your lips look naturally fuller while maintaining proportion with your face. You'll still look like you—just refreshed.

How much filler do I need?

Most clients achieve their desired results with 1ml of filler. First-time clients often start with 0.5-1ml for a natural enhancement. During your consultation, we'll recommend the appropriate amount based on your lips, goals, and facial proportions.

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream before treatment, and most fillers contain lidocaine for comfort. Most clients describe mild pressure and minimal discomfort. The treatment takes only 15-20 minutes.

What about swelling?

Some swelling is normal and expected, typically peaking 24-48 hours after treatment and subsiding within 3-7 days. We provide ice packs and detailed aftercare instructions to minimize swelling. Plan your treatment when you can take it easy for a few days.

How long do lip fillers last?

Lip fillers typically last 6-12 months. The lips are a high-movement area, so filler may metabolize slightly faster than in other facial areas. Most clients schedule maintenance appointments every 6-9 months.

Can I still kiss and eat normally?

Yes! Once initial swelling subsides (3-7 days), your lips will feel soft and natural. You can kiss, eat, drink, and smile normally. The filler integrates with your tissue for natural movement and sensation.

What's the difference between a lip flip and lip filler?

A lip flip uses anti-wrinkle injections to relax muscles around the upper lip, creating a subtle "flip" outward for fuller appearance without adding volume. Lip filler actually adds volume to the lips. Many clients combine both for optimal results.

What if I don't like my results?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved using an enzyme called hyaluronidase. While this is rarely needed, it provides reassurance that lip enhancement is not permanent if you're unhappy with results.
