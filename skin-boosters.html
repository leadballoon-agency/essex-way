What Are Skin Boosters?

Skin boosters are injectable treatments that deeply hydrate and rejuvenate your skin from within. Unlike dermal fillers that add volume, skin boosters work by delivering hyaluronic acid, polynucleotides, or other bioactive compounds directly into the skin to improve texture, elasticity, and overall quality.

Perfect for dull, dehydrated, or aging skin, these treatments stimulate collagen production, enhance skin elasticity, and provide a natural, healthy glow. Results improve over time as your skin becomes more hydrated and revitalized.

Key Benefits

  • Deep skin hydration
  • Improves skin quality
  • Boosts collagen & elastin
  • Reduces fine lines
  • Natural, healthy glow
  • Improves skin texture
  • Long-lasting results
