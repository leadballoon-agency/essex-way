What is the OxiVelour Oxygen Facial?

The Zemits OxiVelour is a revolutionary oxygen therapy treatment that delivers 93-95% pure oxygen directly to your skin through an innovative hands-free dome. This advanced facial treatment harnesses the natural healing power of oxygen to rejuvenate, hydrate, and restore your skin's youthful radiance.

Unlike traditional facials, the OxiVelour creates a hyperbaric-like environment that floods your skin cells with oxygen, boosting cellular metabolism and promoting rapid skin regeneration. The result is visibly brighter, smoother, and more youthful-looking skin with a natural, healthy glow.

Perfect for all skin types and concerns, this relaxing treatment requires zero downtime and delivers immediate, visible results. Experience the transformative power of pure oxygen therapy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an oxygen facial feel like?

The oxygen facial is incredibly relaxing. You'll lie comfortably while the hands-free dome rests over your face, creating a gentle pressurized environment. Most clients describe the sensation as peaceful and calming, with many experiencing deep relaxation during the 30-45 minute treatment.

How quickly will I see results?

Results are visible immediately after your first treatment! Your skin will appear brighter, more hydrated, and noticeably more radiant. Fine lines are softened and your complexion looks refreshed and rejuvenated.

How often should I get oxygen facials?

For optimal results, we recommend a series of 6-8 treatments performed weekly or bi-weekly. After your initial series, monthly maintenance treatments help sustain your glowing results.

Is there any downtime?

No downtime whatsoever! The oxygen facial is completely non-invasive with no redness, peeling, or recovery period. You can return to work or social activities immediately with beautifully glowing skin.

Who is a good candidate for oxygen facials?

Everyone! Oxygen therapy is safe for all skin types, tones, and ages. It's perfect for anyone seeking to improve hydration, reduce fine lines, boost radiance, or simply enjoy a relaxing, rejuvenating facial experience.

Can I combine oxygen facials with other treatments?

Absolutely! Oxygen facials pair beautifully with other treatments like microneedling, dermal fillers, or skin boosters. The oxygen therapy can enhance results and speed healing. Consult with us to create your perfect treatment plan.

What skin concerns does oxygen therapy address?

Oxygen facials are excellent for dehydrated skin, dull complexion, fine lines, wrinkles, uneven tone, enlarged pores, and loss of elasticity. The treatment also helps with acne by killing bacteria and promoting faster healing.
