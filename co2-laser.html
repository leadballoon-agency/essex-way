What is CO2 Laser Treatment?

CO2 laser resurfacing is a revolutionary treatment that uses concentrated beams of light to remove layers of skin with precision. This advanced procedure stimulates collagen production deep within the skin for dramatic, long-lasting rejuvenation.

The treatment is highly effective for reducing wrinkles, treating acne scars, improving skin texture and tone, and addressing sun damage and age spots.

At Essex Way Clinic, we use state-of-the-art CO2 laser technology to deliver safe, effective results with minimal downtime.

Treatment Benefits

  • Treats acne scars & surgical scars
  • Improves skin texture & tone
  • Removes sun damage & age spots

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CO2 laser treatment painful?

We use topical anesthesia to ensure your comfort during the procedure. Most patients experience minimal discomfort, describing it as a warm, prickling sensation.

What is the downtime?

Downtime varies depending on the treatment intensity. Expect 5-10 days of redness and peeling. We provide comprehensive aftercare instructions to support optimal healing.

How long do results last?

Results can last several years with proper skincare and sun protection. The collagen stimulation provides long-lasting improvements in skin quality.

How many treatments will I need?

Many patients see dramatic results after just one treatment. Depending on your concerns and goals, additional sessions may be recommended for optimal results.
