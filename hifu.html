HOW DOES IT WORK?

The Mind-Blowing Science Behind HIFU Ultra 4D

1

PRECISE DELIVERY OF HIFU

We target specific skin depths, ranging from 1.5mm to 4.5mm, by directing ultrasound beams into the skin layers. The ultrasound penetrates the epidermis, dermis, and even the SMAS layer for specific areas like the jawline.

2

FOCUSED ULTRASOUND CONVERGENCE

Once these ultrasound beams reach their depths, they converge at focal points and generate localised heat. This heat kickstarts the production of collagen which is crucial for skin tightening and rejuvenation.

3

COMPREHENSIVE SKIN REJUVENATION

By stimulating collagen in the epidermis, dermis, and even the muscular layer, our process effectively tightens and revitalises the face. This ensures a comprehensive approach to skin rejuvenation.

4

IMMEDIATE AND IMPROVING RESULTS

Although immediate results are visible, the results will gradually improve over the 3-6 months after treatment as the collagen continues to rebuild, providing long-lasting improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will I be able to see results with HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D?

You can expect to see immediate results after a session with HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D, and these results will continue to improve 3-6 months post-procedure.

Will I need any time off work?

No. There is no downtime involved with HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D, meaning you can return to work the very same day.

Are there any side effects?

There are some very rare potential risks to be aware of post-treatment. These include slight redness, tenderness, and swelling. These side effects are minimal and should subside shortly after treatment.

How long does a HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D treatment take, and is it painful?

A typical session can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 90 minutes, depending on the area being treated. Most clients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. Some may feel a slight tingling or warmth as the ultrasound energy is delivered, but this is generally well-tolerated.

What kind of results can I expect from HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D, and how long will they last?

Results can include a noticeable lift and firming of the skin, reduction of wrinkles, and a more contoured facial profile. Improvements can continue to develop for up to 3 months post-treatment. The results are long-lasting, with many clients enjoying the benefits for up to a year or more.

How many HIFU Diamond Ultra 4D treatments will I need?

Many clients see results with just one treatment. However, depending on the degree of skin laxity and individual response to ultrasound energy, some may benefit from additional treatments. During your consultation, a tailored treatment plan will be discussed based on your specific goals.
