What is PRP Therapy?

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy uses your own blood's healing properties to regenerate, repair, and rejuvenate your skin. We extract a small blood sample, concentrate the platelets and growth factors, then inject or apply them to treatment areas to stimulate collagen production, improve skin texture, and promote natural healing.

Highly effective for facial rejuvenation, hair restoration, and scar reduction, PRP is a natural treatment that uses your body's own regenerative capabilities for lasting results.

Key Benefits

  • 100% natural treatment
  • Stimulates collagen production
  • Improves skin texture
  • Reduces fine lines & wrinkles
  • Promotes hair growth
  • Reduces scar appearance
  • Long-lasting results
