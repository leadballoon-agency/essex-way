What is 3D EMLift?

3D EMLift is an advanced device that combines cutting-edge technologies proven to deliver impressive facial rejuvenation and anti-aging results. This innovative system utilizes High Intensity Focused Electromagnetic energy combined with Radio Frequency (RF) to remodel and restore your skin.

High Intensity Focused Electromagnetic energy selectively engages the muscles, enhancing muscle density and quality, restoring and elevating the support of facial features. RF technology effectively increases the stimulation of collagen production and elastin fibers through dermal heating.

This process results in smoother and more youthful-looking skin with a complete treatment taking just 20 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many treatments will I need?

Optimum results are typically achieved with 3-4 sessions spaced 1-2 weeks apart. Your practitioner will create a personalized treatment plan based on your goals.

Is 3D EMLift painful?

No, treatments are painless and comfortable. Most clients describe a gentle warming and tingling sensation during the procedure.

Is there any downtime?

Absolutely none! You can return to your normal activities immediately after treatment. No recovery time needed.

When will I see results?

Many clients see immediate results after their first treatment. Results continue to improve over the following 3-6 months as collagen rebuilds.

How long do results last?

With proper maintenance and follow-up treatments as recommended, results can be long-lasting. The collagen and elastin stimulation provides sustained improvements.

Who is suitable for 3D EMLift?

3D EMLift is suitable for most adults looking to improve facial contour, reduce signs of aging, and achieve a natural lift without surgery.
